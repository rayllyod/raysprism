> "You can do anything" - My Mom

This website is a collection of notes, essays and other random events or things I want to talk about. My name is Ray and I want this to be spot on the internet where I can write about things that interest me and maybe people can read and enjoy too.
Learning and documenting my writing journey. This is to collect my thoughts for future me but also in part to learn the art of writing. Digital media has given us all the opportunity to share our thoughts. I love learning about how other people think and operate daily, so selfishly, I would ask each and every person reading this to start a blog as well. Heavily inspired by [gwern.net](https://gwern.net/) the GOAT.
