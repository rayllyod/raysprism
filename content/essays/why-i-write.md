I don't write here to explain things to other people, at least not primarily — I write to find out whether I actually understand something, which is a different activity that happens to produce readable output as a side effect. The [[now]] page tracks what I'm currently chewing on, the [[changelog]] tracks how the site itself changes, and everything else is whatever I was stuck on that week, including things as mundane as [[deload-weeks]].
