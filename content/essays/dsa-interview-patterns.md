The first dozen interview problems you solve feel like a dozen unrelated puzzles. The next hundred start to rhyme. Sliding window, two pointers, fast-and-slow pointers, monotonic stack — most "new" problems are one of a handful of patterns wearing a different word problem. Recognizing the pattern is most of the work; the code after that is mechanical. [[two-pointer-technique]] is the one I lean on most, mostly because so many array and string problems collapse into it.
