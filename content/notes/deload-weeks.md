title: Deload weeks aren't optional
created: 2026-07-08
tags:

Every time I've skipped a deload because I "felt fine," I've paid for it two or three weeks later with a nagging joint issue that costs more training time than the deload would have. The fatigue that deloads address doesn't show up as feeling tired — it shows up as your knee twinging on a set you've done a hundred times before.
