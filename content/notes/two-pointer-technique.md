Two pointers moving toward each other (sorted array, target sum) versus two pointers moving in the same direction at different speeds (fast/slow, cycle detection) are different enough that I used to mix them up mid-interview. Worth keeping separate mental slots for. See [[dsa-interview-patterns]] for where this fits among the other patterns.
