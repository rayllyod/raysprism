The first time I tried caffeine was actually much later than what people would usually expect. I tried to avoid coffee or tea till I was around 17 years old.
The first time was a lower dosage, probably a cup of tea, which has approximately 50mg of caffeine. It didn't change my life drastically or have an bodily changes. Usually 50-100mg is the required dosage to start noticing its function like alertness and reduction in fatigue.The safe limit for caffeine is around 400mg above which can have side effects of jitterniness and anxiety. As you keep reading, you'll realise that this is a limit I often cross even to this day quite regularly. 
I started abusing this drug (not the worst one I have done tbh, yet still a drug when you cross its limit it can also have sometimes even worse side effects than some of the harder shit) in college. 
I went to college at 18, same as everyone. My college doesn't have much stress, as do most engineering colleges where I am from. So there isn't a good explanation for why I started abusing caffeine other than procrastination and a lot of all-nighters before exams.
At first I used to have just around 4-6 small cups of coffee (diluted with milk and water), 2-3 in the morning and 2-3 in the afternoon. This would amount to just under 150 to 200mg of coffee in my opinion (cant really tell exact amounts due to it being prepared by someone else). This was the ideal amount I think anyone can have and still not be crossing the insane amounts I would reach during mid semester and end semester examinations. I would stay awake for 1-2 days at times (not the longest I have been awake unfortunately, harder shit keeps you awake for 3 days sometimes).
